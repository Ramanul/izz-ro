# Persistent documentary dossiers and explained relations

Goal: deliver plan points 5/6 without changing collection state, generic entity pages or the current recommendation algorithm.
Inputs: a strict, versioned `content/editorial/dossiers.json`, publishable article copies after slug assignment, and optional approved article annotation relations.
Outputs: persistent `/dosar/<slug>/` view models, explained article relations, explicit subject links, and a machine-readable preparation report.
Acceptance: an expired article leaves no broken link; source documents and approved summaries survive; event and publication dates are separate; a changed target fingerprint hides its relation; co-occurrence never implies causality; withdrawn/unavailable evidence is visible and suppresses dependent current assertions.
Integration: call `prepare_dossiers` before any output deletion; render its pages before sitemap/search/file accounting; attach returned relation lists to article copies and subject links only to actual subject pages. No new AI calls or service.
Files: `content/editorial/dossiers.json`, `generator/dossiers.py`, `generator/editorial.py`, `generator/render.py`, `templates/{article,subject,dossier,_editorial_relations}.html`, `tests/test_editorial_dossiers.py`, `.github/workflows/editorial-frontend-audit.yml`, `specs/{editorial-dossiers,STATE}.md`.
Boundaries: `documentary` identifies assisted documentary examination, never human approval. The real pilot is one official Sector 1 notice; it does not certify platform availability or independent corroboration. A dossier is a bounded history, not the complete history of its subject. Reviewers use public identifiers, not email addresses. Search indexes current title/question/summary, event text and uncertainty content; fixed disclosures, source metadata and historical summaries remain visible but outside the search body. Navigation templates consume validated preparation output; they are not general URL sanitizers for arbitrary callers.

## Public integration contract

`load_dossiers(path=None)` returns `{id: record}` (an absent catalog is empty; malformed content raises `EditorialError`). `prepare_dossiers(catalog, articles, annotations=None, now=None)` returns `(pages, article_relations, subject_dossiers, report)` without mutating any input.
Article annotations must first pass `editorial.validate_annotations`, as they do in `render.build`; this function additionally validates dossier records and relation structure. It is not a replacement annotation/evidence schema validator for arbitrary direct callers.

- `pages` contains approved or withdrawn dossier views; `path`, `title`, `summary`, `reviewed_at`, `documents`, `events`, `history`, `uncertainties`, `relations`, `article_links`, and `indexable` are ready for autoescaped templates. Withdrawn pages are explanatory and excluded from search/indexing. Their public content retains the title and review metadata to identify the route plus the withdrawal note; it does not repeat the original question, coverage scope, summary, evidence or dependent assertions.
- `article_relations` maps an article `annotation_key` to ready-to-render relation items. Article relations come from a fresh approved annotation's `relations` array; dossier `article_links` also provide reviewed context links to their fingerprint-matching source article.
- `subject_dossiers` maps an explicitly approved subject slug to dossier links; the caller only displays these on subject pages it actually emits. No names are automatically merged.
- `report` counts emitted/indexable/withdrawn dossiers and lists suppressed article references or target/source changes. It contains public keys and reason codes, no private message content.

Relations require type, target, explanation, document/evidence IDs, documentary/human review metadata and `identity_basis`. `affects` additionally requires `effect_status=observed|projected` and `effect_evidence_ids`. Article targets require `target_fingerprint`; dossier targets require the target dossier content fingerprint. Changing substantive target content demands renewed review. `co_occurrence` is a separate type with a fixed visible noncausal disclaimer.
All distinct relation and effect evidence is linked publicly in first-mentioned order, without duplicate links; inactive evidence in either list suppresses the relation. An optional relation review `input_fingerprint`, when supplied, must still be a valid SHA-256 string.

Article source links and relation source/target approval compare their fingerprints to the displayed article fields (`source_fingerprint` of the corrected copy). They never reuse an earlier raw-input fingerprint after a presentation correction.

Dossier article references keep the approved article key, fingerprint, and explanation. An article may expire; its reference is then omitted from navigation while the dossier's document references remain. Events retain an optional `event_at` independently of `published_at`; events with missing event dates never inherit publication dates. Only an approved evidence-backed history item may change the current summary, and the latest recorded version must equal the dossier summary.
History is optional: an empty array means no version snapshots have been recorded; a singleton version 1 is the initial evidence-backed baseline, not a correction. Version 2 onward records changes while retaining all prior versions, and only these changes display `Ce s-a schimbat`. Validation enforces consecutive versions and the current-summary match; it cannot recover or prove the absence of an earlier editorial version outside the catalog.
The current correction pipeline removes withdrawn articles before preparation. Text and county presentation corrections invalidate old dossier article links and relation source/target fingerprints; navigation returns only after examination of the displayed revision. No legacy `publication`/`editorial_input_fingerprint` fields are used. The county override enters the fingerprint only when present, preserving every unchanged source fingerprint.

## Verification responsibility

Unit and template tests cover schema rejection, unsafe URLs/HTML, immutability, expiration, same-name disambiguation, target changes, noncausal association, withdrawn documents and summary correction history. The integrating executor verifies site build, sitemap/search inclusion, browser experience and frontend audit on the combined branch. Rollback removes navigation/render integration while retaining the versioned catalog; a published withdrawn dossier retains an explanatory route.
The frontend workflow compares three runs of each code revision using the exact same PR-head `data/articles.json` snapshot, copied only to its temporary baseline checkout; corpus changes are excluded from that code comparison.
